Privacy notice

Last updated: 2026-10-28

Who we are

This website is run by CREOVA ("we"). SEEN is a CREOVA product. To contact us, see the Contact section at the end of this page.

What this website collects

Nothing on purpose. The site has no accounts, forms, comments, cookies, analytics or advertising, and it loads no scripts, fonts or images from other companies. One small script from this site runs the "Make a SEEN" toy in your browser; what you choose is not stored or sent anywhere.

What our hosting provider sees

Like any website, our hosting provider receives your IP address and standard request details (such as the page you asked for, your browser type and the time) so it can deliver the page and keep the service secure. We do not use this information to identify you. The provider handles it under its own privacy terms.

Links to other sites

This site links to creova.one and to the SEEN demo app. They are separate sites with their own practices.

The demo app

The demo runs on sample stories and sample accounts. What you create in it stays in your browser's own storage on your device and is not sent to us. To display fonts and some story cover images, the demo loads them from Google Fonts and Unsplash, so those services can see your IP address. Clearing your browser's site data for the demo removes what it stored.

Accounts in the beta

When real accounts launch with the beta, the app will have its own privacy notice and terms, published before anyone can sign up. This notice covers the website only.

Your rights and questions

Depending on where you live, you may have rights over your personal information. If you have questions about this notice or about your information, contact us (see Contact). If you email us, for example to ask for an invite, we receive your email address and message and use them only to reply.

Changes

We will post changes here and update the date above.
