Good to know

A few honest answers.

Can I try SEEN now?

By invitation. We are sharing the demo app with a small group while we build. Ask for an invite and we will reply. It runs on sample stories and sample accounts, and what you create in it stays in your own browser.

When does the beta open?

We are planning Beta 1 for January 2027. The date is to be confirmed, and we will update this page when it is.

Is SEEN only in English?

No. The app works in English, French and Spanish, and so does this site.

Who makes SEEN?

CREOVA, a cultural storytelling studio in Ontario.

Can I share my own stories?

Creator tools are part of the plan for the beta. We will say more when they are ready.
